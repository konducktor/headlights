A game for **GMTK Game Jam 2026**

# Headlights
Sometimes, shadows of the past creep into the present. Especially when you are inside the mind of a troubled girl. Troubled by what? Well, it’s your job to find out. And while you do that you’ll have to face choices whether to gain power to defend against these haunting memories at the cost of health or remain helpless. 

And so, the countdown to the tragic accident begins at 16…
